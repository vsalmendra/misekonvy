# 2026-11-01 · Mindenszentek

**User's instructions:** All Saints, Eucharistic prayer I by default. Readings of the solemnity
(not of the Sunday).

**Calendar:** Sunday, 1 November: Mindenszentek – FÜ (solemnity; igenaptár "Mindenszentek – FÜ"),
replacing the 31st Sunday in Ordinary Time. White. Gloria and Creed.

**Assembly**
- Own Mass of the solemnity: 1991 missal (PPEK 335, `Misekonyv_1.pdf`), November 1., pp. 620-621
  (PPEK page numbers): entrance antiphon, collect, prayer over the offerings, preface, communion
  antiphon (Mt 5,8-10), prayer after communion. The collect has no "Vagy:".
  Latin: Missale Romanum cum lectionibus 1977, vol. IV, Die 1 novembris, Omnium Sanctorum,
  pp. 753-757 (OCR fixed by hand: "Fflium" → "Filium", "qusesumus" → "quaesumus", "quadragmta" →
  "quadraginta", "ssecula" → "saecula", "fflii" → "filii", "Vemte" → "Venite", "ccelis" → "caelis",
  "Epistolse primse" → "Epistolae primae"; "Per Dominum." / "Per Christum." expanded; no accents,
  as printed).
- **Creed added to the library** (first Creed in the app): `ordo.toml` `creed` — Hungarian from the
  1991 missal, Ordo Missae 15., pp. 355-356 (the Nicene–Constantinopolitan Creed; the "Vagy:"
  Apostles' Creed not taken; OCR "üdvösségürmért" → "üdvösségünkért"; the 1991 rubrics "Mindnyájan
  mélyen meghajolnak:" / "Felegyenesednek:" kept as rubric lines). `ordo-latin.toml` `creed` —
  Latin Missale Romanum 2008 (ordo.pallotyni.pl, Ordo Missae, Liturgia verbi 18.), paired paragraph
  by paragraph; the rubric lines span both columns (Hungarian wording). The build puts it after the
  homily in both parts.
- Readings, psalm, alleluia: of the solemnity, from `src/cycle/fix-11-01.json`, checked against
  igenaptár 2026-11-01: Jel 7,2-4.9-14; Zsolt 23,1-2.3-4ab.5-6; Mt 11,28; 1Jn 3,1-3; Mt 5,1-12a.
  Two readings → `reading1` ("Olvasmány a Jelenések könyvéből") and `reading2` ("Szentlecke Szent
  János apostol első leveléből"). Psalm asterisks and tone numbers dropped.
  Reading 1 summary: igenaptár has "meg sem **t**ehetett számlálni" — a typo (the text itself reads
  "meg sem lehetett számlálni"); corrected to "lehetett".
  Latin: 1977 vol. IV, pp. 753-756 (Latin paragraphs regrouped to stand opposite the Hungarian
  ones: Jel 7,13-14 one paragraph; 1Jn 3,1-3 one paragraph after "Carissimi:").
- Prayer of the faithful: igenaptár 2026-11-01 ("20. Mindenszentek ünnepére"; Pap/Lektor/Hívek
  dropped). Its 5th petition (for the dead) is part of the set, not the optional one, so it has no rubric.
- Preface: `mindenszentek` (new in `prefaces.toml`; "Anyánknak, a mennyei Jeruzsálemnek dicsősége",
  1991 pp. 620-621, singing aids stripped, "(zengjük:)" dropped → "mondjuk:"). Latin "De gloria
  Matris nostrae Ierusalem" from the 1977 volume pp. 756-757 (not on ordo.pallotyni.pl: its Proper
  of Saints stops in July), accents added as in the Missale Romanum (like `kereszt`). Then EP II and
  EP IV's own (added by the app).
- Eucharistic prayer I by default.

**Open points**
- All propers, the preface and the **Creed** are from the 1991 missal, not checked against the new
  missal (2023). No official online text of the new missal's Creed was found; the 1991 wording is
  used ("leszállott", "feltámadott", "az idő kezdete előtt", "…Anyaszentegyházban").
- igenaptár 2026-11-01 gives propers that differ from the 1991 missal — possibly the new missal's
  wording (not verified, so not used):
  - entrance: "Örvendezzünk mindnyájan az Úrban / ünnepi napot ülvén Mindenszentek tiszteletére, /
    kiknek ünnepén örülnek az angyalok, / és magasztalják az Istennek Fiát." (1991: "Mindenszenteket
    ünnepelve, / akiknek ünnepén ujjonganak az angyalok, / és magasztalják Isten Fiát.");
  - communion: "Boldogok a békességszerzők" (1991: "Boldogok a békességben élők");
  - collect, prayer over the offerings, preface, prayer after communion: the same as 1991 (line
    breaks aside).
- The Latin psalm response and alleluia are from the 1977 missal (not checked against the Lectionarium).
