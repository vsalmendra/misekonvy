# 2026-10-22 · Szent II. János Pál pápa

**User's instructions:** Szent II. János Pál. Default Eucharistic prayer: III.

**Calendar** (igenaptár month page): 29. évközi hét csütörtök; Szent II. János Pál pápa —
választható emléknap. White. No Gloria, no Creed.

**Assembly**
- Readings, psalm, alleluia: igenaptár, 2026-10-22 (Ef 3,14-21; Zsolt 32,1-2.4-5.11-12.18-19;
  Fil 3,8-9; Lk 12,49-53). Psalm asterisks, "E:/H:" labels and the tone number removed;
  "Irgalmában" lowercased. The two-line gospel summary joined.
- Prayer of the faithful: **igenaptár's** (the lectionary's year-II set still not available online).
  Optional 6th petition (for a deceased person) kept with its rubric.
- Mass: Lelkipásztorok miséi, **1. pápák vagy püspökök miséje** (1991 missal, pp. 672-673), all
  prayers and antiphons. Collect: the one "pápák ünnepén"; the "Vagy:" for bishops does not apply.
- Collect: the common's only — following the user's decision on 2026-10-21 (Boldog Károly). Saint
  John Paul II has an own collect in the Missale Romanum (added 2014: "Irgalomban gazdag Istenünk,
  aki az egész Egyház vezetésére hívtad…"), but no reliable copy of the official Hungarian text was
  found online (Vatican News and the Tábori Püspökség quote different wordings). Add it as the first
  option if the user wants and gives the text.
- Prefaces: lelkipásztorok (default), szentek-1, szentek-2, then EP II and EP IV's own.
- Eucharistic prayer: III by default.
- Names: "Szent II. János Pál (pápa / -t)"; Latin "beatum Ioannem Paulum", "beatus Ioannes Paulus"
  (the 1977 missal's "beatus" for any saint), preface "sancti Ioánnis Pauli".
- Latin: lectionary, Missale Romanum cum lectionibus 1977, vol. IV, pp. 354-356; Commune pastorum 1,
  pp. 865-866 (OCR corrected by hand, no accents). Preface of pastors: Missale Romanum 2008
  (ordo.pallotyni.pl).

**Open points**
- Common and preface of pastors are the 1991 Hungarian wording, not checked against the new missal.
