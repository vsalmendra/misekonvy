# 2026-10-26 · Gyászmise több vagy minden elhunytért

**User's instructions:** Mass for many or all deceased. Eucharistic prayer II.

**Calendar** (igenaptár): 30. évközi hét hétfő, ferial day — a Mass for the dead is allowed.
Violet ("lila"; black is also allowed). No Gloria, no Creed.

**Assembly**
- Readings, psalm, alleluia: ferial, igenaptár 2026-10-26 (Ef 4,32–5,8; Zsolt 1,1-2.3.4 és 6;
  Jn 17,17b és a; Lk 13,10-17). Psalm asterisks/daggers, "E:/H:" labels and the tone number removed;
  "gúnyolódok" → "gúnyolódók". The gospel starts without "Abban az időben", as on igenaptár.
  (The lectionary for the dead could be used instead — not done, by the ferial-readings rule.)
- Prayer of the faithful: **igenaptár's** (lectionary year-II set not available online). Optional
  6th petition (for one deceased person) kept with its rubric.
- Mass: Gyászmisék, III. Megemlékező gyászmisék, **C. több (vagy minden) elhunytért** (1991 missal,
  pp. 846-847) — the first formula for several dead. New common `src/library/commons/elhunytak.toml`
  holds C, D and E. Only the first collect and prayer after communion (user, 2026-10-25: no "Vagy:"
  alternatives); antiphons have none.
- Names: the collect's "N.-nek és N.-nek" is dropped ("Add kegyelmedet gyermekeidnek, …"), because
  the Mass is for all the dead; in Latin the names are optional, "famulis tuis (N. et N.)".
  If the Mass is said for named dead, set `names` to put them in.
- Prefaces: megholtak I (default) – V, then EP II and EP IV's own. Hungarian 1991 (pp. 406-410):
  singing aids removed, also "(a te)" in preface I; typos fixed ("igazsákos", "dicsőséded").
  Latin: Missale Romanum 2008 (ordo.pallotyni.pl).
- Eucharistic prayer: II by default. Its memento of the dead is the ordinary one; the special
  insertion of EP II for Masses for the dead is for one named deceased, so it is not used.
- Latin: lectionary, Missale Romanum cum lectionibus 1977, vol. IV, pp. 376-378 (Hebdomada XXX,
  feria II, anno II); Mass C, pp. 1664-1666 (OCR corrected by hand, no accents).

**Open points**
- Mass C and the prefaces of the dead are the 1991 Hungarian wording, not checked against the new missal.
