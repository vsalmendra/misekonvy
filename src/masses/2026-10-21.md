# 2026-10-21 · Boldog IV. Károly király

**User's instructions:** continuous readings, prayers for Boldog IV. Károly király, Mass for the
saints. Prefaces for the saints I and II. Default Eucharistic prayer: III.

**Calendar** (igenaptár month page): 29. évközi hét szerda; Boldog IV. Károly király — választható
emléknap. White. No Gloria, no Creed.

**Assembly**
- Readings, psalm, alleluia, prayer of the faithful: igenaptár, 2026-10-21 (Ef 3,2-12;
  Iz 12,2-3.4bcd.5-6; Mt 24,42a.44; Lk 12,39-48). Psalm asterisks, "E:/H:" labels and the tone
  number removed. The optional 6th petition (for a deceased person) kept with its rubric.
- Collect: own collect of Boldog Károly. Not in the 1991 missal (beatified 2004). Text from
  ferencesekzeg.hu ("Boldog IV. Károly király emléknapja"); Magyar Kurír ("Boldog IV. Károly
  király") has the same prayer with "viszontagságos úton a földi királyságból" and a short
  conclusion. Used the parish's order and the full conclusion, "Te" lowercased. **Verify against
  the printed Hungarian proper.**
- Entrance antiphon, prayer over the offerings, communion antiphon, prayer after communion:
  Szentek általános miséi, **formula 1** (1991 missal, p. 690). The formula gives two communion
  antiphons (Zsolt 67,4 / "Vagy:" Lk 12,37): both offered, the first selected.
- Prefaces: szentek-1 (default), szentek-2, then EP II and EP IV's own.
- Eucharistic prayer: III by default.
- No Latin section.

**Open points**
- Commons and saints' prefaces are the 1991 wording, not yet checked against the new missal.
