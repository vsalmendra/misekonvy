# 2026-10-30 · Votív mise a szent keresztről

**User's instructions:** votive Mass of the Holy Cross, Eucharistic prayer I by default, readings of
the day.

**Calendar:** 30. évközi hét péntek (no memorial). Red (votive of the Holy Cross). No Gloria, no Creed.

**Assembly**
- Votive Mass "A szent keresztről": the missal gives no own formula — the rubric says "Mise a szent
  kereszt felmagasztalásának ünnepéről, piros színben" (1991 missal p. 817, "Votív (megemlékező)
  misék" 2.; its "647." is the printed book's page). So the propers are those of 14 September,
  A Szent Kereszt felmagasztalása: 1991 missal pp. 594-595 (PPEK page numbers). Without the
  Gloria (feast only).
  Latin: Missale Romanum cum lectionibus 1977, vol. IV, Missae votivae, 2. De mysterio sanctae
  Crucis, pp. 1508-1509 ("Dicitur, cum colore rubro, Missa de festo Exaltationis sanctae Crucis");
  the same texts as 14 Sept., pp. 695-699. "Per Dominum." / "Per Christum." / "Qui vivis." expanded.
- Readings, psalm, alleluia: of the day (30. évközi hét péntek, II. év): Fil 1,1-11; Zsolt 110;
  Jn 10,27; Lk 14,1-6 — from `src/cycle/evkozi-30-5-II.json` (igenaptár; psalm "Vagy: Alleluja."
  lines dropped). Latin: 1977 vol. IV, pp. 394-395 (the Latin reading has no "Fratres:" line: "—"
  opposite "Testvéreim!"; it is "Initium Epistolae…").
- Prayer of the faithful: igenaptár 2026-10-30 (the weekday's).
- Prefaces: `kereszt` (default; "A dicsőséges szent kereszt győzelme", 1991 missal p. 595; Latin
  "De victoria crucis gloriosae" from the 1977 missal p. 1509, accents added as in the Missale
  Romanum) and `szenvedes-1` (Urunk szenvedéséről I., which the rubric also allows; 1991 p. 370,
  Latin Missale Romanum 2008, ordo.pallotyni.pl), then EP II and EP IV's own.
- Eucharistic prayer I by default.

**Open points**
- All Hungarian propers from the 1991 missal, not yet checked against the new missal.
- The Holy Cross preface is not on ordo.pallotyni.pl (its Proper of Saints stops at August); Latin
  from the 1977 OCR.
