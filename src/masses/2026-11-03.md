# 2026-11-03 · Porres Szent Márton szerzetes

**User's instructions:** "Szent Márton, Eucharistic prayer 3" — the Mass of Porres Szent Márton,
default Eucharistic prayer III.

**Calendar** (igenaptár, month page and `nap/index.php?holnap=2026-11-03`, checked 2026-11-02):
3. kedd: 31. évközi hét kedd (Fil 2,5-11; Zsolt 21,26b-27.28-30a.31-32; Lk 14,15-24);
"Választható emléknap: Porres Szent Márton szerzetes". The day page gives only the weekday
readings and prayer of the faithful — no propers of the saint. The 1991 missal's calendar also has
"3 Porres Szent Márton szerzetes" (no rank = optional memorial). White. No Gloria, no Creed.

**Assembly**
- Mass: the 1991 missal, November 3 (PPEK 335, p. 624): "Szerzetesek miséje" + own collect.
  Missale Romanum cum lectionibus 1977, vol. IV, p. 761: "De Communi sanctorum (pro religiosis)"
  + collecta propria. Common: Szentek általános miséi, **7. Szerzetesek miséje** — the first of the
  two "Szerzetesek" formulas (7 and 8), PPEK p. 696; Latin "7. Pro religiosis", 1977 vol. IV,
  pp. 945-946. Added to `src/library/commons/szentek-altalanos.toml` as `[7]` (Hungarian + Latin);
  the abbots' alternative collect ("Vagy: (apátok miséiben)") is stored as `collect_b` but not offered.
- Collect: two options — **"Saját"** (the saint's own, 1991 p. 624; Latin 1977 p. 761; typo of the
  PPEK text "égi dicsősére" → "égi dicsőségre") and **"Közös"** (common 7). The proper is in the
  Roman Missal itself (general calendar), so it is official.
- Entrance antiphon (Zsolt 15,5-6), prayer over the offerings, communion antiphon (Vö. Mt 19,27-29),
  prayer after communion: common 7. Names: "Szent N.-nek" → "Porres Szent Mártonnak",
  "Szent N." → "Porres Szent Márton"; Latin "beatus/beato/beati N." → "Martinus/Martino/Martini".
- Readings, psalm, alleluia, gospel: ferial, 31. évközi hét kedd, II. év (`src/cycle/evkozi-31-2-II.json`,
  identical to igenaptár 2026-11-03): Fil 2,5-11; Zsolt 21,26b-27.28-30a.31-32; Mt 11,28; Lk 14,15-24.
  Psalm asterisks, † (line broken there), "E:/H:" labels and the tone number ("8 G. tónus") removed.
  Latin: 1977 vol. IV, pp. 416-418 (Anno secundo; OCR fixed by hand: "arbitratm" → "arbitratus",
  "obdediens" → "oboediens", ligatures æ/œ written ae/oe, no accents). The Latin psalm strophes follow
  the Latin lectionary (v. 29 "Quoniam Domini est regnum…" is in the Latin, not in the Hungarian;
  "Et semen meum serviet ipsi." printed so in all three places of vol. IV).
- Prayer of the faithful: **igenaptár's** (2026-11-03) — to be replaced by the lectionary's (year II)
  when a source is available. "N. pápáért" → "Leó pápáért"; the optional 6th petition (for a deceased
  person) kept with its rubric.
- Prefaces: szentek-1 (default), szentek-2 (the missal suggests no proper preface; the preface "a
  szüzekről és a szerzetesekről" is not suggested for the day), then EP II and EP IV's own.
- Eucharistic prayer: III by default.
