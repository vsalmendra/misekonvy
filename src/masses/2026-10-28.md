# 2026-10-28 · Szent Simon és Szent Júdás Tádé apostolok

**User's instructions:** today's Mass, St Jude (Simon and Jude). Both the feast readings and the
ordinary (weekday) ones, selectable by a button. Both prefaces of the apostles plus the usual ones.
Prayer of the faithful of the feast, if any.

**Calendar** (igenaptár): Szent Simon és Szent Júdás apostolok – Ü (feast). Red. Gloria, no Creed.

**Assembly**
- Own Mass of the feast (1991 missal, pp. 618-620): entrance antiphon, collect, prayer over the
  offerings, communion antiphon (Jn 14,23), prayer after communion. Latin: Missale Romanum cum
  lectionibus 1977, vol. IV, pp. 749-752 (OCR corrected by hand, no accents).
- Gloria: added to the library (`ordo.toml`, Hungarian from katolikus.hu "A szentmise állandó részei",
  Latin from Missale Romanum 2008); not yet checked against the new missal.
- Readings, two sets (buttons, the feast's selected):
  - **Ünnepi:** igenaptár 2026-10-28 (Ef 2,19-22; Zsolt 18,2-3.4-5; alleluia "Téged, Isten, dicsérünk…";
    Lk 6,12-19). Latin: the same 1977 volume.
  - **Hétköznapi** (30. évközi hét szerda, II. év): Ef 6,1-9; Zsolt 144,10-11.12-13ab.13cd-14;
    2Tessz 2,14; Lk 13,22-30. Hungarian: igenaptár's archived year 2024 (`/2024/nap/index.php?holnap=2024-10-30`,
    when this liturgical day was not a feast). Latin: 1977 volume IV, pp. 385-387.
- Prayer of the faithful: igenaptár's "30. Az apostolokról" set given for the feast; "Szent N. (és N.)"
  → "Szent Simon és Szent Júdás Tádé", "N. pápánkat" → "Leó pápánkat"; the optional "(N. testvérünk és
  összes)" dropped.
- Prefaces: apostolok-1 (default), apostolok-2, then EP II and EP IV's own.
- Eucharistic prayer III by default (as on the previous days).
